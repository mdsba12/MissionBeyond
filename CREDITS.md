## Real missions shown in the debrief

- [Landsat 9](https://science.nasa.gov/mission/landsat-9/): A NASA and USGS satellite launched in 2021 that images Earth's land surface from low Earth orbit.
- [Lunar Reconnaissance Orbiter](https://science.nasa.gov/mission/lro/): Has mapped the Moon from lunar orbit since 2009.
- [MAVEN](https://science.nasa.gov/mission/maven/): Launched in 2013 to study how Mars' upper atmosphere escapes to space.
- [Mars Reconnaissance Orbiter](https://science.nasa.gov/mission/mars-reconnaissance-orbiter/): Launched in 2005; it aerobraked into its science orbit and carries a 3 m high-gain dish.
- [James Webb Space Telescope](https://science.nasa.gov/mission/webb/): An infrared observatory launched in December 2021 that orbits Sun–Earth L2, about 1.5 million km from Earth.
- [Juno](https://science.nasa.gov/mission/juno/): A solar-powered Jupiter orbiter launched in 2011; its arrays make about 500 W at Jupiter.
- [Europa Clipper](https://science.nasa.gov/mission/europa-clipper/): Launched in October 2024 to study Jupiter's moon Europa through repeated flybys, on solar power.
